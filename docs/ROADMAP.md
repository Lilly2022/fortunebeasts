# Roadmap 🗺️

The concept art vision (养娃 · 护娃 · 偷娃 · 运娃 — raise, protect, steal, transport)
is much bigger than the MVP. This is the order we grow into it, one playable
phase at a time. **Rule: never start a phase until the previous one is fun.**

## ✅ Phase 0 — MVP (this repo, done)

The core loop: own beasts → earn coins → vault or expose → steal / chase →
caravan → split fair or betray. 5 beasts, 8 plots, 1 caravan route, trust +
Oathbreaker, beginner protection.

## Phase 1 — Retention basics

- **DataStore saving** — keep coins, trust, and beasts between sessions
- **Collection board** — "Collected: X/Y" screen; owning one of each matters
- **Squish mechanic** 🫳 — squeeze your beast for a small instant coin bonus
  (30s cooldown). Pure serotonin, straight from the concept art.
- Sounds + squash-and-stretch animations on steal, capture, payout

## Phase 2 — Vehicles & real transport (运娃)

From the vehicle posters: caravans stop being a walking escort and become driving.

- Rentable vehicles with real trade-offs (speed / capacity / defense / cost):
  handcart (free) → van → courier truck → armored carrier → freight truck
- Load your own beasts as cargo; more cargo = bigger delivery payout
- Used-car market with hidden breakdown chance (风险: cheap ≠ good)
- Vehicle upgrades: engine, tires, cargo box, safe, paint, lucky charms

## Phase 3 — Insurance & risk (买保险)

The turtle-accountant's insurance desk from the art:

- **Beast insurance** — partial payout when you're robbed
- **Transport insurance** — covers ambush/breakdown losses on routes
- **Betrayal insurance** — compensation when a caravan partner grabs extra
- Tiers: Basic 30% / Standard 50% / Full 70% payout

## Phase 4 — City routes (伦敦 · 首尔 · 北京 · 香港 · 上海)

Each concept-art city becomes a delivery route with its own personality:

| Route | Flavor | Profile |
|---|---|---|
| 🇬🇧 London | fog bridges, tolls, police patrols | high cost, steady profit |
| 🇰🇷 Seoul | neon streets, trendy vehicles | style + speed |
| 🏮 Beijing | hutong maze, checkpoint dodging | steady, evasion gameplay |
| 🇭🇰 Hong Kong | slopes, harbor ferry transfers | short + fast turnover |
| 🌃 Shanghai | finance district, market-crash events | highest risk/reward |

Plus city-exclusive beasts (8 per city, per the posters) to collect.

## Phase 5 — Factions & social (Lantern Pact / Iron Crown / Pearl River)

- Faction choice with identity bonuses: Lantern Pact (trust & fair-split),
  Iron Crown (raid & defense), Pearl River (caravan & trade)
- Guild caravans (3–4 players), shared vaults, faction leaderboards
- Bounty board: top Oathbreakers become wanted 通缉 targets

## Phase 6 — Monetization (only after it's fun)

Cosmetics first, never pay-to-win: beast skins, vehicle paint, base decorations,
pets, emotes. No paid random draws for players under Roblox's policy thresholds.

---

### Concept-art naming note

The MVP uses the "Fortune Beasts" cast (Tax Rat, Gold Frog, Vault Turtle,
Trust Owl, Debt Dragon). The fruit-baby cast from the art (草莓88, 蜜桃66,
金娃宝宝...) maps cleanly onto the same system later — a beast is just a config
entry + a model, so we can ship the fruit-baby lineup as a themed collection
(or re-skin entirely) without touching game logic.
